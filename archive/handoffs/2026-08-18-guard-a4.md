## The attribution choice (option (a) vs (b))

Neither as scoped — a **narrowed (b)**. Findings are attributed to every agent that receives
persona *instruction* text, read at runtime from `core.orchestrator._HEAD_LAYER_AGENTS` rather
than hardcoded, so a new head-layer agent extends the scan automatically. Literal (b) — "every
agent that can load persona files" — means all 14, since every specialist loads `goals.yaml`;
one defect reported 14 times is the 34-false-positives-to-1 ratio `_tools_named_in`'s docstring
exists to prevent. Literal (a) was rejected as too tight: it couples to loader wiring per file.
`load_config()` is the single loader of persona markdown and is reached only by the head layer,
so reading that one constant gets (a)'s precision with (b)'s drift-safety.
