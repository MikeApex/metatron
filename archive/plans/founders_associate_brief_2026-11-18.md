# Founder's Associate (Contract): Position Brief

November 2026

## What we're building

We're building a personal AI companion for the long arc of a life. It helps a person live in line with what they actually value, and it becomes more useful the better it knows them. A working first version already exists and is in use. The next stage is turning it into something a first group of people can't imagine doing without.

Most software that touches daily life is built to capture attention or to squeeze out more output. This product starts from the opposite premise. What people want from their lives is rarely more productivity. They want happiness that lasts, contentment with where they are, fulfillment in what they spend their time on, and close relationships with the people who matter to them. Rest, connection and depth are the point, not inefficiencies to be optimized away. The only measure that matters is whether someone's life goes better over years, not whether they opened the app today.

That premise produces unusually interesting problems:
- How do you learn what a person values without putting words in their mouth?
- How do you help someone invest in their relationships without turning them into tasks?
- How do you help without nagging?
- How do you earn trust with the most personal information someone has?
- How do you tell whether any of it is working?

Those questions are the work.

## The role

A solo founder is hiring an associate on a 90 to 120 day contract to help take the product from a working MVP to a first validated group of users. The role is part time to full time, by agreement.

This is not an assistant role. The associate works directly with the founder on the central question of what to build and who to build it for, and helps build it. It is the company's first hire.

**One line for forwarding:** Paid 90 to 120 day remote contract, part time or full time, for a numerate STEM student or recent graduate with a real interest in human behavior and wellbeing. They would work directly with a solo founder on user research, measurement and product build for a personal AI companion built around happiness, fulfillment and relationships rather than engagement. There is a defined path to a full-time Founder's Associate role with equity, leading toward Chief Operating Officer.

### Path to Founder's Associate

The contract is the first stage of a defined progression, not a standalone piece of work.

1. **Contract, days 1 to 120.** Base rate and milestone bonus. Milestones are set jointly in the first week and reviewed at day 30 and day 60.
2. **Conversion.** At the end of the contract, a full-time Founder's Associate offer that includes equity. The terms are discussed before the contract begins, so nobody negotiates blind.
3. **Beyond.** The Founder's Associate role is designed as the route to Chief Operating Officer as the organization grows. The scope grows with the business rather than being redefined around it.

## Responsibilities

The work runs on a learn, build, measure cycle: find out what people actually need, build against it, measure what happens, and repeat. The associate works across the whole cycle rather than owning one slice of it.

- **User research.** Recruit and interview prospective users about how they actually spend their time, the relationships that matter to them, and what a better life would look like in their own terms. Turn what they say into findings that change what gets built.
- **Audience definition.** Narrow a wide field of possible users down to the one group most likely to adopt first, test that judgment against evidence, and map how to expand beyond it.
- **Onboarding design.** Work out what takes a new user from first contact to genuine value, and refine it.
- **Product build.** Contribute code to core features alongside the founder.
- **Measurement.** Build the measurement that tells us whether each decision worked. This is harder than usual because time-in-app is not the goal. The question is whether people are happier, more fulfilled and more connected, and the measurement has to respect the privacy of what users share.
- **User growth.** Build a repeatable way to reach and recruit the target users as the base widens.
- **Positioning.** Develop language that makes the right person recognize themselves in the product, and turn it into a promotional pipeline.

How the work is split across these areas is not fixed in advance. Tasks are reassigned as development requires, sometimes week to week, and the associate is expected to move with the product's needs rather than follow a set sequence.

## Traits we select for

This section matters more than the one above it. Background can be checked from a CV. These traits cannot, and they decide whether the contract converts.

**Curiosity over credentials.** We want someone who finds the problem interesting enough to keep pulling at it when nobody is watching. The tell is unprompted depth: they have gone further into something than any assignment required.

**Broad native intelligence, broadly read.** In a single week the work covers interviewing, statistics, code, writing and positioning. We need range and fast transfer between domains more than depth in one. We also mean breadth of reading in the older sense: reading that runs well outside the person's discipline, philosophy included. This product is about how people decide what a good life is and whether they are living it. Understanding how people reason, what they value, what makes them content and how they justify themselves is directly useful in that work.

**Care for the user over the metric.** Plenty of products are built to be hard to put down. We want someone who finds that uninteresting, or troubling, and who would rather solve the harder problem of building something that earns its place in a person's life.

**Executors, not planners.** A good plan delivered in week two beats a better plan delivered in week six. We want someone whose instinct is to put something in front of a user and find out.

**Comfort without structure.** There is no syllabus, no rubric, and often no correct answer. The associate will spend much of the contract working out what the right question is. We want someone who finds open problems energizing and who sets their own standard when nobody else has set one.

**Intellectual honesty.** Willing to report evidence that kills their own preferred idea, and to tell the founder when the founder is wrong.

### A screening question for referrers

If you are wondering whether a student fits, one question separates candidates well. *Ask them about something they investigated purely because they wanted to know, with no assessment attached. Listen for how far they took it and whether they changed their mind partway through.*

## What the associate gets

This is stated plainly because it is the part a professor will weigh when deciding whether to put a strong student forward.

- **An apprenticeship with the founder.** Daily contact, nobody in between, and a running explanation of why decisions are made the way they are. By the end of the contract the associate should understand how a business actually runs: how revenue is generated, how costs behave, how customers are found and kept, and how the pieces connect. That understanding is the most transferable thing on this list, and it is the foundation of the operating role the position leads to.
- **Ownership of real decisions.** The associate's findings shape which users the company builds for. This is not a simulation, and it is not an internship project with a presentation at the end.
- **A complete cycle, start to finish.** Few early-career roles let one person work across research, build, measurement and growth on the same product within a single quarter. Most specialize immediately.
- **A method that transfers.** Evidence-driven product development, user research and measurement design carry into any product role, any research career, and into founding something.
- **Work that is worth doing.** A chance to show that technology aimed at people's happiness and relationships, rather than their attention, can be built and can find its users.
- **A real path forward.** A full-time Founder's Associate role with equity if it works on both sides, on a track toward Chief Operating Officer. A candid reference and a genuine body of work if it does not.
