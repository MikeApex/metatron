# Before you sign up

Metatron is an early, unfinished personal assistant. Please read this before you ask for an
account.

- **What you tell it is processed by Google.** Your messages, and anything Metatron keeps about
  you to help you, are sent to Google's Vertex AI to be answered. Under Google's terms this is
  not used to train their models, and it is logged only if Google's abuse filter flags it —
  then kept for up to 90 days.
- **Some replies read aloud go to Microsoft.** If you have replies read aloud in a language its
  own voice can't speak, the text is sent to Microsoft's online voice service to be spoken.
- **The developer can read everything.** Mike built and runs Metatron. He can read everything
  you tell it and everything it keeps about you.
- **It is early and incomplete.** Things will go wrong, and some of what it says will be wrong.
  Don't rely on it for anything important, medical or urgent.

You can stop using it at any time; ask Mike and your account is switched off.
