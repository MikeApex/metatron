# Read my calendar and mail

Connecting your Google account lets Metatron read your calendar and your email — **including
what other people have written to you** — so it can plan your day and help with your messages.
It can also add events to your calendar and send email from this address when you ask it to,
and it will check with you first before anything is sent.

What it reads is processed by Google's Vertex AI, on the same terms as everything else you tell
Metatron. Mike, as its developer, can read it too.

Your app password is stored **only on Metatron's server, never in a backup**, where Mike can
reach it. Disconnecting deletes it.

Later, Metatron may offer to fill in a contact's details from their public profile — it will
ask you separately before it does.
